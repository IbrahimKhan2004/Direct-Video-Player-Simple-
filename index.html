Experience seamless video streaming with our URL Video Player Online. It's designed to play videos directly from any downloadable link in your browser.

Note: Ensure the video URL has permissive CORS headers and supports range requests to allow playback and seeking.

Usage Tips:

  • Tap to play or pause. Double-tap the left or right side to skip 10 seconds. Swipe for seek and volume on mobile.
  • Right-click (or long-press on mobile) the player for speed, aspect ratio, audio and subtitle tracks, HDR, Picture-in-Picture and more.
  • Playback depends on the source URL allowing cross-origin requests (CORS) and range requests.

What's Supported

Containers
MKV, MP4, M4V, AVI, MOV, FLV, MPEG-TS, WebM
Video
H.264, HEVC (H.265), AV1, VP9, VP8, MPEG-4 (DivX/Xvid), MPEG-2, H.266 (VVC)
Audio
AAC, Opus, FLAC, MP3, AC3, E-AC3, DTS, TrueHD
HDR
HDR10, HLG, Dolby Vision (profile 8)
Subtitles
Built-in SRT, ASS/SSA, WebVTT, PGS, DVB. Local files: .vtt (use the toggle above)
Streaming
Direct links (range requests), HLS (.m3u8), MPEG-DASH (.mpd)
Tracks
Multiple audio tracks (Hindi is picked automatically when found) and subtitle tracks
Resolution
Up to 4K. Playback speed 0.25x to 2x (capped at 1.5x for 4K and above)

Features

  • Hardware-accelerated decoding (WebCodecs) with automatic software fallback
  • Resume playback where you left off, per video link
  • Seek preview thumbnails, Picture-in-Picture, fullscreen, snapshot, loop, rotate
  • Stable volume (loudness normalization) and stats for nerds
  • Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded

Powered by MoviPlayer. Demuxed by FFmpeg (WebAssembly), decoded by WebCodecs.

Recommended Browsers: Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome (Safari 18+ and Firefox 130+ also work)
